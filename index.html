How to read a claim

Billed is what the provider charged. Allowed is the plan rate for that service. Plan paid is what Verity paid. You may owe is the allowed amount minus what the plan paid, before any payment you already made at the visit.

If a claim looks wrong, open it and choose Ask About This Claim. Your question carries the claim number with it, so you do not have to retype it. Appeals need a short note about what should change and why.
